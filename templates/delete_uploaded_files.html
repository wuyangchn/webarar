Delete Uploaded Files

Files uploaded by users to Webarar will be renamed with a unique string, and automatically and permanently deleted within 24 to 48 hours. If you wish to manually delete files in advance, please click "Delete". Webarar identifies users of different terminals by recording cookies. If you clear your browser cookies, new user id will be generated upon your next visit, which may prevent you from viewing or manually deleting files uploaded previously. However, there is no need to worry—we reiterate that Webarar will permanently delete any files you uploaded to the server within 24 to 48 hours.
